Trading Workflow

From setup to review.

A simple process for turning each trade into a measured decision.

  1. 01 Plan Trade

    Define the setup, entry, stop, target, and account context.

  2. 02 Calculate Risk

    Convert the plan into lot size, loss exposure, and reward profile.

  3. 03 Execute Trade

    Keep prop-firm guardrails visible before the order goes live.

  4. 04 Journal Result

    Record outcome, PnL, balance movement, notes, and lessons.

  5. 05 Analyze Performance

    Review statistics and account progress to improve the next trade.
